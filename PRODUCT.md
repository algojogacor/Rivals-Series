# PRODUCT CONTEXT: RIVALS SERIES SEASON 1 (RS S1) OFFICIAL WEBSITE

## 1. Product Truth & Identity
- **Product Name:** Rivals Series Season 1 (RS S1) Official Website
- **Nature of Product:** The authoritative digital publication, live competition records center, and operations hub for an independent, private two-participant eFootball 1v1 Dream Team league.
- **Season:** Season 1 (RS S1)
- **Game Engine:** eFootball™ Friend Match 1v1, Dream Team
- **Participants:** Exactly two participants (configurable as Participant A and Participant B; no fabricated identities).
- **Matchdays:** Exactly 20 regular matchdays (MD1–MD10: Group Stage; MD11–MD20: Knockout Stage).

## 2. Target Users & Permissions
1. **Public Visitors:**
   - Any visitor following the competition.
   - Read-only access to official standings, confirmed match results, goal scorers, individual and team awards, Goal of the Season clips, public disciplinary logs, and the rules summary.
   - Zero access to private evidence files, unconfirmed match reports, or unrevealed private ballots.
2. **Participant A & Participant B:**
   - The only two official competitors.
   - Authenticated operations: submit match reports, confirm or dispute opponent's reports, register 26-card rosters and mid-season changes, attest Trending/DSP compliance, track Indonesian player half-credits, nominate up to two Goal of the Season entries, and cast locked ranked votes.
3. **Competition Administrator:**
   - One designated participant who handles operational data entry.
   - Has operational input rights, but **zero authority to unilaterally override** the opponent's confirmation, silence disputes, alter confirmed scores without an audit trail, or invent sanctions.

## 3. Core Jobs To Be Done (JTBD)
1. **Authoritative League Table:** View official standings calculated strictly from confirmed matches, including goal difference ($GD = GF - GA$), tiebreakers, Indonesian quota deductions, and potential early champion mathematical verification ($G_{adj} > 3 \times R$).
2. **Streamlined Match Reporting:** Report match results, scores (regulation, ET, PK shootout), virtual scorers, and starting goalkeeper clean-sheet conditions without spreadsheet maintenance.
3. **Dual Confirmation & Tamper-Proof Audit:** Ensure every score report requires dual confirmation (or 24-hour unchallenged administrative confirmation) and logs every correction with timestamps, reasons, and article references.
4. **Quota & Roster Compliance:** Track the 26-card squad limit (no duplicate virtual players `base_player_id` per squad, minimum 2 Trending cards), mid-season MD10-11 transfer window, Trending 2-player simultaneous full-half rule per matchday, and 5 full-half Indonesian player credits.
5. **Private Goal of the Season Voting:** Secret ballot ranking (4-3-2-1 points) where votes remain concealed until both participants lock, then automatically resolves ties via non-owner scores.
6. **Dispute & Reconstruction Records:** Document connection interruptions, own-goal reconstruction events, digital coin toss Technical Wins, and export standardized dispute packages for the designated AI Adjudicator.

## 4. Design Stance & Modes
- **Surface Mode (Public):** `Read` & `Operate` — Sports editorial authority, competition record, disciplined publication tone. It reads like the official digital gazette of a formal football league, not a commercial SaaS landing page or a flashy gaming clan portal.
- **Surface Mode (Private Control):** `Operate` — Fast, accessible, utilitarian operations console with high density, clear status indicators, and keyboard-friendly form submission.
- **Language & Voice:** Natural, crisp Indonesian for UI narrative and explanations; standard international football terminology for recognized competition labels (Matchday, Golden Boot, Golden Glove, Best Defence, Technical Win, Goal of the Season).
- **Anti-AI Writing Standards:** No empty buzzwords (*seamless, robust, elevate, unlock*), no em dashes (`—`), no fake metrics, no artificial testimonials, and varied, authentic human phrasing.
